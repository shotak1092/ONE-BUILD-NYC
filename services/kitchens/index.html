Kitchens

One crew. One schedule.

A kitchen touches every trade at once: gas and water lines, dedicated appliance circuits, venting, under-cabinet and scene lighting, and often built-in audio. We plan all of it on day one so the walls only get closed once.

What's included

  • Layout and cabinetry installation
  • Countertops, backsplash and tile
  • Sink, dishwasher, gas and water lines
  • Dedicated appliance circuits and panel work
  • Recessed, under-cabinet and pendant lighting
  • Lighting control scenes and keypads
  • Built-in ceiling speakers
  • Laundry and pantry build-outs
