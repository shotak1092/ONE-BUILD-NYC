Licensed New York electricians handle panels, new circuits and every line that feeds the lighting, shades and AV, so power and control are designed as one system instead of patched together.

  • Panel upgrades and new circuits
  • Dedicated lines for appliances and equipment

  • Shade power and motor circuits
  • Outdoor and landscape lighting power
  • EV charger circuits
  • Permits, filings and inspections
