Questions & Answers

Lighting Control questions.

What is lighting control?

Lighting control replaces rows of switches with keypads and scenes. One button can set every light in a room, or the whole house, to the right level for cooking, dinner, a movie or leaving.

Can lighting control work with my existing fixtures?

In most cases, yes, as long as the fixtures are dimmable and compatible with the dimmers. We check fixture and driver compatibility before installation.

Do you design the lighting layout too?

Yes. We lay out recessed, track, cove, accent and landscape lighting with the room, then wire, install and program it.
