Bathrooms

One crew. One schedule.

From compact co-op baths to marble primary suites, we take bathrooms down to the studs and rebuild them with licensed plumbing, proper waterproofing and lighting that actually works at the mirror.

What's included

  • Full demolition and waterproofing
  • Walk-in showers, tubs and glass enclosures
  • Wall and floor tile, niches and benches
  • Vanities, medicine cabinets and mirrors
  • Licensed plumbing and fixture installation
  • Vanity, shower and recessed lighting
  • Exhaust fans and GFCI circuits
  • Smart toilets, heated floors and in-ceiling speakers
