Home Theater

One crew. One schedule.

We build the room and the system together: framing for the screen and speakers, dedicated circuits, acoustic treatment, lighting scenes, then calibrated audio and video. From a clean media wall to a full LED-wall lounge.

What's included

  • Dedicated theaters and media rooms
  • Projection screens and large-format displays
  • LED video walls
  • In-wall and in-ceiling surround audio
  • Acoustic treatment and seating layout
  • Lighting scenes and blackout shades
  • Concealed wiring and equipment racks
  • Calibration and one-remote control
