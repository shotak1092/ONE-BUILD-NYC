Smart Home

One crew. One schedule.

We design and install the systems that make a home run itself: whole-home Wi-Fi and network, multi-room audio, video distribution, cameras, lighting and shades, all on one app and a few clean keypads. Wiring goes in during rough-in, so nothing is surface-mounted later.

What's included

  • Whole-home Wi-Fi and network
  • Equipment racks and structured wiring
  • Multi-room audio and video
  • Lighting and shade integration
  • Security cameras
  • Keypads, remotes and app control
  • Pre-wire during construction
  • Programming and owner training
