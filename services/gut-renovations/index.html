Gut Renovations

One crew. One schedule.

A gut renovation is where coordination matters most. With the walls open, plumbing, electrical and low-voltage go in together, get inspected together, and are documented before anything is closed up.

What's included

  • Demolition down to the structure
  • New framing and layout changes
  • Plumbing supply, drain and venting rough-in
  • New panel, circuits and lighting rough-in
  • Low-voltage, network and speaker pre-wire
  • Insulation, drywall and finishes
  • Permits, filings and inspections
  • Final commissioning and walkthrough
