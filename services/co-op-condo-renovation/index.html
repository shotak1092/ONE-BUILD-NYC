Why buildings are different

The building is a party to your renovation.

In a co-op or condo, the board and managing agent approve your plans before work starts, set the hours your crew can work, decide when the freight elevator is yours, and hold a deposit until the job is signed off. A leak or a dust complaint becomes the building's problem, which is why boards look hard at who is doing the plumbing and electrical.

At One Build NYC, construction, licensed plumbing, licensed electrical and low-voltage work are under one contract. That means one insurance package, one set of license documents and one contact for the managing agent.

Board packagePlans, scope, schedule, licenses and insurance certificates assembled for the managing agent and the building's reviewing architect.
DOB filingsPlumbing and electrical permits filed by licensed professionals; structural or layout changes filed with drawings.
Building rulesWork hours, elevator bookings, hallway protection and debris removal planned before demo.
Close-outInspections, sign-offs and the paperwork the board needs to return your deposit.
How it runs

From alteration agreement to sign-off.

  1. IWalkthroughEvery trade looks at the space, the risers and the alteration agreement with you.
  2. IIPlans & proposalLayout checked against building rules such as wet-over-dry, then one scope and price.
  3. IIIBoard & DOBBoard package submitted; asbestos assessment and DOB filings prepared once the board signs off.
  4. IVBuildDemolition, rough-in, inspections and finishes inside the building's hours.
  5. VSign-offFinal inspections, building walk-through and close-out documents.

Questions

Co-op and condo renovation questions.

What is an alteration agreement?

It's the contract between you and your co-op or condo board that sets the rules for your renovation: what has to be submitted, who reviews it, insurance requirements, work hours, deposits and how the work is inspected and closed out.

What is the wet-over-dry rule?

Many buildings don't allow a kitchen or bathroom to be moved above a neighbor's bedroom or living room, because a leak would land in dry living space. Not every building has it, and some grant exceptions with extra waterproofing, so read your alteration agreement first.

Does the board have to approve my DOB permit filing?

Since February 2, 2026, when a condo unit owner or co-op shareholder files on DOB NOW: Build, the board is a stakeholder and a board representative attests that the filing is authorized. Settle board approval first.

Is an asbestos check required?

For buildings built before April 1, 1987, DOB requires an asbestos assessment by a certified asbestos investigator before it issues a permit, unless an exemption applies (for example, no existing materials are disturbed).

Who provides the certificate of insurance?

We do. Boards typically require certificates naming the building and the managing agent, from the general contractor and from the licensed plumbing and electrical trades. Ours come from one company.

Sources: NYC DOB asbestos requirements, DOB service notice on condo/co-op board stakeholders.
