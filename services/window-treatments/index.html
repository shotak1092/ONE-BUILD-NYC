Questions & Answers

Window Treatments questions.

Do motorized shades need wiring?

Hardwired shades need power or low-voltage at the top of each window, which is easiest during construction. Battery-powered shades are available for finished spaces.

Can shades be recessed into the ceiling?

Yes. Recessed pockets are framed before the ceiling goes up, so the shade disappears when it's open. That decision needs to be made during framing.

Can shades run on a schedule?

Yes. Shades can follow sunrise and sunset, run on schedules, and be grouped into scenes with your lighting.
