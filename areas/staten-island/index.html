Questions

Common questions from Staten Island clients.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom or kitchen renovation in NYC?

Often, yes. Work that moves or adds plumbing, adds or changes electrical circuits, or changes walls usually needs permits filed with the NYC Department of Buildings by licensed professionals. Purely cosmetic work, like replacing a vanity in the same spot or repainting, often doesn't. We confirm exactly what your project needs at the walkthrough and handle the filings.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

It depends on scope, building approvals and material lead times. A straightforward full-bathroom gut renovation typically takes a few weeks of on-site work once materials are in hand; co-op approvals or custom items can add time before work starts. You get a written schedule with the proposal.

How much will my project cost?

Every project is priced after a walkthrough, because layouts, building rules and finishes change the number a lot. You get one written proposal with the scope broken out by trade, so you can see exactly where the money goes before anything starts.
