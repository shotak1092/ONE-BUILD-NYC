Renovating in Upper West Side

What the buildings here ask for.

The Upper West Side is prewar co-op country along Central Park West, West End Avenue and Riverside Drive, with brownstone and rowhouse blocks between them. Large parts of the neighborhood are in designated historic districts.

Classic six and classic seven layouts often get reworked: a closed kitchen opened to the dining room, a maid's room turned into a bath or office, old two-wire circuits replaced. Each of those touches plumbing or electrical, so we file with licensed trades and line up the board package at the same time.

Questions

Upper West Side renovation questions.

Is my Upper West Side building landmarked?

Many buildings here sit in historic districts. You can look up any address on the Landmarks Preservation Commission's map. If it is designated, interior work that needs a DOB permit also needs an LPC permit.

Can I add a bathroom to a prewar apartment?

Often, if the new bathroom can drain to an existing riser and the board allows it. Location relative to the riser and the wet-over-dry rule usually decides it. We check both before design.
