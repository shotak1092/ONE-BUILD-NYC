Renovating in Greenwich Village

What the buildings here ask for.

Greenwich Village is one of the city's largest historic districts, with rowhouses, walk-ups and prewar co-ops on narrow streets. Small floor plates, old framing and limited access shape every job here.

Deliveries, debris removal and parking take planning on Village streets, and many buildings have no freight elevator. We sequence the trades so the stairs are used efficiently and neighbors see as little disruption as possible.

Questions

Greenwich Village renovation questions.

How do you renovate in a walk-up with no elevator?

We plan deliveries and debris removal around the stairs, protect common hallways, and stage materials so fewer trips are needed. Your building's rules on hours and hallway protection apply.

Do I need Landmarks approval for an interior renovation in the Village?

If your building is in a historic district or is an individual landmark, interior work that requires a DOB permit also requires an LPC permit, along with any work that affects the exterior, such as new vents.
